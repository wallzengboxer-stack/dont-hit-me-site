Support — Don't Hit Me! Dodge Show

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email wallzengboxer@gmail.com — please include your device model and iOS version.

Quick help

  • Swipe LEFT or RIGHT to slip, DOWN to duck, UP to jump. You can slip and jump or duck at the same time.
  • Tap right before a big crate hits to smash it. When the counter meter is full, tap to catch a throw and send it back.
  • Every kind of throw has its own warning sound two beats ahead. After a fake, the cue sound is always the real throw.
  • Too hard? Turn on Assist mode in Settings. Sound and picture out of step? Use the audio latency calibration in Settings.
  • Language: Settings → Language (14 languages).
  • Game Center: sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center to see the leaderboard.
