Privacy Policy — Don't Hit Me! Dodge Show

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Don't Hit Me! Dodge Show ("the app", "we") is a game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app handles.

We do not collect your personal information

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to third parties. It works fully offline.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, best scores, settings (such as language and sound), coins, hats, stickers and play statistics are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID; the app only learns whether the purchase is owned so it can unlock the content. You can restore the purchase on another device with the "Restore purchase" button.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's Game Center service so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
